The alphabet with a birthday 🎂
Hangul was created in 1446 by King Sejong the Great, so ordinary people could read and write.
Korea even has a national holiday for it: 한글날, October 9.

Say this in Korea for a guaranteed smile:
한글은 정말 과학적이에요. Hangul is really scientific.
