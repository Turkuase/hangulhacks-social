Learn 1 word, get a verb for free 🎁
Korean builds hundreds of verbs as noun + 하다 (to do):
공부 (studying) → 공부해요 (I study)
요리 (cooking) → 요리해요 (I cook)
사랑 (love) → 사랑해요 (I love you)
