"To" a person isn't 에 in Korean 👀
에 = to a place
한테 / 에게 = to a person
친구한테 전화했어요 (chin-gu-han-te jeon-hwa-hae-sseo-yo) = I called my friend.
Add 서 and it flips to "from": 엄마한테서 = from mom.
