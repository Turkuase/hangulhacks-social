Ever noticed Korean announcements end in 니다? ✈️
That's the formal speech level:
갑니다 (gam-ni-da) = I go
먹습니다 (meok-sseum-ni-da) = I eat
학생입니다 = I am a student
Add 까 to ask: 어디 갑니까?
You already know one: 감사합니다!
