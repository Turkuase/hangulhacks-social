How to invite someone in Korean ☕
-(으)ㄹ까요? = Shall we…?
같이 갈까요? (ga-chi gal-kka-yo) = Shall we go together?
뭐 먹을까요? = What shall we eat?
It also offers help: 도와드릴까요? = Shall I help you?
