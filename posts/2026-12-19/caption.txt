"Let's" in Korean, two ways 🙌
Formal: 시작합시다 (si-ja-kap-ssi-da) = Let's begin.
Casual: 가자! = Let's go!
One warning: -ㅂ시다 can sound bossy to elders. Use 갈까요? with them instead.
