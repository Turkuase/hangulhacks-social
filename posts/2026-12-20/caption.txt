One ending, two jobs: -아/어서 🔗
Because: 늦어서 죄송해요 (neu-jeo-seo joe-song-hae-yo) = Sorry I'm late.
And then: 친구를 만나서 커피를 마셨어요 = I met a friend and had coffee.
