Two ways to say "because". Which one? 🤔
If your sentence ends in a request or "let's", use -(으)니까:
비가 오니까 우산 가져가세요 = It's raining, so take an umbrella.
For thanks and sorry, use -아서: 늦어서 죄송해요.
