"I have to" in Korean 📝
아/어 form + 야 해요 (or 야 돼요 in speech)
가야 해요 (ga-ya hae-yo) = I have to go.
공부해야 돼요 = I have to study.
Year-end classic: 대청소해야 돼요 = I have to do a big clean 🧹
