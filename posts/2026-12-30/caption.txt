"You must not" in Korean 🚫
stem + (으)면 안 돼요 = if you do it, it's not okay
늦으면 안 돼요 (neu-jeu-myeon an dwae-yo) = You mustn't be late.
As a question, it's how Koreans beg sweetly:
하나만 더 먹으면 안 돼요? = Can't I have just one more? 🥺
